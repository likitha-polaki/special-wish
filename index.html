To the world, you are my lecturer and teacher, but in my heart, you are also like an elder sister. ♡

Life introduces us to many people, but only a few leave a meaningful mark on our hearts. For me, you are one of those people, Kavya Akka. ❤️ Kavya Mam… For Ever, I’m Your Student! 🥹🌸

Mam, naa life lo jarigina konni vishayalu, naa manasulo inni rojulu daachukunna feelings anni meetho share chesukovalani anukuntunnanu. 🥹❤️

Nenu 6 months baby ga unnappude maa amma ki job vachindhi. Appati nunchi nenu ekkuvaga maa nannamma daggare periganu. Maa nannamma government teacher avvadam valla, nenu kuda thanatho paatu school ki vellipoyedhaanni. Ala naa childhood antha nannamma thone gadichindhi, almost 6th class varaku.

School lo unnappudu nenu topper ni, studies lo kuda manchi knowledge undedhi. Kaani naa life lo oka wrong decision valla antha sudden ga maaripoyindhi. 10th class varaku nannu nenu deniki panikiranu ane feeling tho chala rojulu struggle ayyanu. 🥺

10th ayyaka studies meedha focus taggipoyindhi. Em chadavalo, em decide chesukovalo kuda naaku ardham ayyedhi kaadhu. Vere vallu em chepthe adhe follow ayye daanni. Naa decisions nenu theesukune antha confidence appudu naaku ledhu mam.

APRJC, POLYCET, AGRI CET ila chala entrance exams raasanu. Results kosam wait chesthu time waste avvakudadhu ani Aditya Junior College lo join ayyanu. Kaani naaku Inter chadavali ani anipinchaledhu. Agriculture side vellalani undhi ani maa parents ki cheppanu. Vallu kuda naa decision ki oppukunnaru.

Results vachina roju AGRI CET, POLYCET counselling dates okate roju vachayi. Agriculture college allotment gurinchi alochisthunna time lo, naaku support ga unna sir maa parents tho maatladi, counselling ki velli colleges chuddam, nachithe decide cheddam ani chepparu. Ala memu counselling ki vellamu.

Kaani mam… naa life lo oka kotha journey start avuthundhani appudu naaku theliyadhu. ❤️

October 22, 2022 na memu first time ISTS college ki vachamu. ❤️

Mam, aa date naaku just oka date kaadhu. Adhi naa life lo oka kotha journey start ayina roju. Naa life lo kotha environment, kotha friends, kotha people… anni kothaga start ayyayi.

Aa roju open auditorium lo “Kothaga Kothaga” movie promotion jaruguthundhi. Movie peru lagaane naa life kuda kothaga start ayyindhi mam. 🥹🌸

Starting lone meetho naa feelings share chesukovalani anukunna, kaani correct time dorakaledhu. Andhuke ippudu ee webpage dwara meeku cheppalanukuntunnanu. 🥹❤️
